--- mn-edit:loop:4:body
+ Once on the ground the harvester automatically collects (**Harvests**) whatever it landed on. It can then be issued movement instructions in **STEP** actions, these require clear co-ordinates. Harvesters only move up/down/left/right and require clear co-ordinates. Any unreachable co-ordinate issued is automatically rejected by the game as illegal once your policy is submitted, so it is possible to make one error in co-ordinates that then invalidates a whole chain of steps! Harvesters automatically harvest any square they step onto, be that red, blue or green. Harvested red turns to green (called synthetic-green to differentiate it from green that was naturally on the board at the start) meaning any other harvester stepping into that square is penalised.
