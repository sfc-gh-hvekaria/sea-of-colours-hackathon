TURN LAB

a frozen night, opened in the real interface
Once you are in: the AGENT tab has an invoke control for each seat — pick who plays it and press go, and you will see it plan. Invoke one seat and the board waits; invoke both and the night resolves and plays out exactly as a live night does. The [ RED ] [ RHL ] [ OBS ] buttons isolate each seat's vision, and the plan overlay draws the shape of what each seat queued.

Ordnance is handed out here rather than being part of the board, so the same night can be opened armed and empty and the only difference between the two runs is the rack. Arm one seat and not the other if what you want to know is what a weapon is worth.

The turn is a throwaway copy. Whatever happens to it, the frozen board is untouched — open it again and you are back at the same night with nobody's orders in.
