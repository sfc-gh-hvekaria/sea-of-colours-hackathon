v8What v8 is

The worldview release. v7 proved the agent could reliably finish a plan and reason before it. v8's investigation found the reasoning was operating on an incomplete picture — the agent literally never saw one of the most important things that happened to it. v8 is a true fork of the feeding layer: it owns the prompt, the doctrine, and the rules module, while reusing v7's proven computation layer (hint compilers, sanitizer, wishlist, memory, weapon inference, recorder) unchanged.

Scope note — v8 vs v9

v8 stays on the current stack (haiku + the contained two-call split) and spends its effort on data, doctrine, and observability. The separate bet — bounded native thinking on a thinking-capable model (sonnet) via reasoning.max_tokens — is v9. Fixing what the agent knows comes before making it think harder about it.

01The investigation — a three-way pile-up the agent never saw

The seed-42 arena produced a perfect case study. On day 2 a pure-RED beacon broadcast near (2,13). All three agents fixated on it. What happened next was almost comedic.

day 3 — everyone rushes the exact cell, everyone crashes. SIMULTANEOUS DROP COLLISION at (2,9): all three harvesters (p1+p2+p3) dropped on the same broadcast cell and mutually annihilated. All stayed orbital-damaged, 0 cargo. Nobody banked the pure.
day 4 — v8 drops offset at (2,14) onto the seam → harvested 6 parcels (incl. purity 210/245). v7 dropped on the exact beacon (2,9) again → 0. v6's drop was rejected (no live sensor).
day 5 — v8 ships the haul: +2349 (two purity-255 pures + 245 + 210 + 161). Meanwhile v7 and v6 collided on (2,9) again. v8 dropped offset at (2,12) and banked 6 more.

In that earlier arena (v8 still on the pre-fork shim) v8 came out ahead. But that raised the sharp question that drove this whole release: did it actually understand the pile-up, or did it get lucky? We read its own chain-of-thought. The answer was damning.

The smoking gun — day 7, verbatim from the thinker's CoT

Racing the final-night beacon, the thinker recites the exact risk — and dives onto the exact cell anyway:

- Mitigation: the hot drop lands on the beacon itself (not adjacent), so if both
  harvesters land on the same cell in the same hour, both take damage and return
  with ZERO parcels. This is a LOSS.
...
FINAL CALL:
- POSTURE: redsign_race
- STRATEGY:
  1. Launch probe at (4,8) hour 1
  2. Hot-drop harvester_p1 at (2,9) hour 2   # the exact advertised beacon cell

It knew the collision rule and still chose the mutual-kill cell. Why? Because on the nights it actually collided, the agent was never told it had collided.

02The root cause — a silently-dropped channel

The engine records every collision: collision_marks["x:y"] = {day, owners}, the public ring the frontend renders. But the agent's "last night" recap is built by view._last_night_recap, whose free-text attribution filter (_is_my_row) keys on the player's slug at the start of a log caption. The collision frame is logged with owner=None and a caption that filter drops. So:

what the agent saw

"banked 0"

No cause. So it confabulated one — a "thin seam," or "held in hoard, not lost." The grounded-reflection loop, ironically, made the lie confident.

the truth on the map

a three-way collision ring on (2,9)

Public, fog-safe, sitting in collision_marks — just never surfaced into the reflection channel the agent reads.

The keystone insight

You cannot reason your way out of a mistake you were never told you made. Before making the agent think harder (v9), we had to make sure it could see what happened to it. That is the whole thesis of v8.

03The engineering — a restructured worldview

v8 rebuilds the prompt around three explicit pillars so the agent (and the thinker especially) has a clean scaffold, then fills the gaps the investigation exposed.

PillarContains
SECTION 1
THE GAME		engine RULES (physics) + DOCTRINE (core + state-triggered appendices, incl. the new redsign-poker book) + the STRATEGIST DIRECTIVE handoff.
SECTION 2
THE BOARD NOW		your state, visible RED, drop-legal zones, fog+echo, ENEMY PROBES (new), opponent intel, OPPONENT WEAPONS + WEAPON GEOMETRY (new), the hint menu.
SECTION 3
LAST NIGHT		the engine record — now including COLLISIONS (the keystone) — plus the grounded REFLECT block that names a collision loss as LOST, not "held".

The keystone (§4e) — the collision channel

A new fog-safe my_collisions channel in view._last_night_recap, sourced directly from collision_marks (self-party only — surfaced only when this seat is in the owners list). The v8 LAST NIGHT block renders it explicitly, and the v8 REFLECT block prepends a hard loss-framing line the model must acknowledge:

# SECTION 3 — what the agent now reads after a pile-up
my_collisions (1) — YOU banked ZERO here:
  - harvester COLLIDED at [2,9] with ['p2','p3'] — returned orbital
    damaged, 0 cargo (cargo LOST, NOT held in hoard). The advertised
    cell was a mutual-kill zone.

# REFLECT
COLLISION LOSS: a harvester of yours collided at [2,9] with ['p2','p3']
  and banked ZERO — this cargo is LOST, not held. ... next time drop
  OFFSET onto the seam instead of the advertised cell.

The doctrine (§5) — redsign poker

A new state-triggered appendix that rides with any live redsign. It names the beacon a honeypot, prescribes the winning read (drop offset onto the seam, not the advertised cell), and scales the advice with seat count (more opponents → higher collision odds on the beacon → offset + backup probe + a second harvester are worth more). This is the doctrine that turns the day-7 quote from a tragedy into a play.

§4c · Enemy probes, first-class

Public enemy probe launches promoted from a buried ! WATCHED annotation to a standing block on every night (age + source) — for route-around, staging, supersede, and understanding that a rival can hot-drop into these disks (the collision risk on a shared cell).

§4d · Weapon geometry

A constants block (EMP radius-2 / 3 missiles / 8h cloud, chaff 3h) rendered whenever a seat is armed — so EMP is reasoned about as a targeted orbital strike with known blast, not a distance-from-a-cluster gamble.

§4b · Weapons, reframed

Fired = fact; un-fired build estimate = estimate. Weapons are orbital strikes that reach anywhere — "they're clustered far away, low risk" is named as a misread. Reaction gated to the seat that actually holds the weapon.

Contained split, native

The reasoning-first thinker + moves-first mover are on by default (no env shim). TABULA_V8_SINGLE=1 reverts to mover-only for an A/B.

05Did the fix work? — the reflection now names the collision

The point of v8 is comprehension, so the validation is a comprehension test, not a scoreboard. In a fresh seed-42 arena all three agents piled onto the day-1 bluesign hot-drop and mutually collided at (36,20). Here is v8's day-2 thinker, verbatim — reading the collision it was previously blind to:

# v8, day 2 — the reflection that used to say "banked 0 · thin seam"
Day 1 executed a hot drop (probe 34,18 → drop 36,20 → 6-step chain).
The harvester collided at [36,20] with opponents p2 and p3, returning
0 cargo.
...
- (34,18) — this is the probe that collided with us yesterday.
- Probe B at (34,23) is NEAR the contested zone. High risk of collision.
- Better to play safe and probe the frontier at a different region.
The fix, demonstrated

It names the collision (cell + which opponents), classifies it as a 0-cargo loss (not "held in hoard"), and — crucially — changes the next move because of it, steering away from the contested zone. That is the exact "once it understands what happened, it plays the board better" loop the whole release was built for. Before v8, this same event produced a confabulated "thin seam."

The redsign-poker doctrine shows up too: on the contested night v8's thinker dropped its second harvester offset — "(1,10), offset from harvester_p1 to avoid friendly collision" — instead of stacking both on the beacon.

06On the scoreboard — and why one seed isn't the story

Two runs of the identical seed-42 three-way arena (scripts/run_versus.py), same map, same seats — different outcomes:

Runv8 (p1)v7 (p2)v6 (p3)What decided it
A (pre-fork shim)3728275−100v8 took the seam offset; others collided on the beacon
B (worldview fork)4335542584all three collided day 1; v6's orbit banked the recovery

The swing is enormous for every seat (v6 alone went −100 → 2584). That is the honest headline: a single 7-night, three-stochastic-agent season is dominated by variance — one early three-way collision or one orbit-economy roll reorders the whole table. We do not claim v8 reliably out-scores v7/v6 from this; a proper A/B needs the full seed set (42 / 7 / 99 / 2024), repeated. What is robust across both runs is the mechanism above: v8 sees collisions and reasons about them, and no turn fell back (0 fallbacks all season, ~37–52s/turn — no truncation or latency regression from the restructure).

06The "aha" moments

Recites-the-risk, dives-anyway

The day-7 CoT proved the failure wasn't ignorance of the rule — it was ignorance of its own history. A perfect argument for fixing data before depth.

The bug was a dropped channel

The collision was public and fog-safe the whole time. A slug-matching attribution filter silently discarded it. One missing channel, seasons of confabulated reflections.

"Held, not lost" became a hazard

v6's helpful hoard-vs-shipped framing was wrong on a collision night — nothing entered the hoard. Grounded reflection must be grounded in all the outcomes, including the ones with no owner tag.

Doctrine scales with seats

The honeypot gets deadlier with more opponents, so the offset/backup/second-harvester advice is worth more in a crowd — the opposite of "play it safe when contested".

07What's next — v9

v8 fixed what the agent knows. v9 is the bet on making it think: swap the mover to a thinking-capable model (sonnet) and turn up the second dial from the containment fix — a bounded reasoning.max_tokens pass on the same contained inference path. The parked v7 split failed because the think was unbounded; here it's a hard sub-budget on a separate channel. v9 ships only if the reasoning trace demonstrably changes the moves and clears the wallclock/cost gates against v6/v7/v8.
